# ramayana.com growth plan, 8 November 2026

Research from Plausible, Search Console, Ahrefs, Keyword Planner, Google Trends, autocomplete and live results pages. Raw data and full reports are in the session scratchpad (research/traffic.md, keywords.md, serp-intent.md, design-audit.md).

## Where we are after 10 days

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Visitors since launch (30 Oct to 8 Nov) | 2,006 |
| Daily visitors, 30 Oct to 7 Nov | 22 to 522 |
| Google clicks, week on week | 32 to 444 |
| Play Store clicks | 232 visitors, 11.6% of all |
| Largest source | ChatGPT, 43% of visitors, 20% click to the app |

Google brings visitors mainly through brand searches and the home page. Chaupai pages earn the most non-brand impressions (4,793) but convert poorly to clicks (93) because they did not serve lyrics intent.

## Shipped on 8 November

- Chaupai pages rebuilt for lyrics intent: Hinglish lyrics, word by word meaning, the scene, the teaching, the full passage and an FAQ with FAQ markup, in English and Hindi.
- New stuti pages: Bhaye Pragat Kripala, Jai Jai Giriraj Kishori, Rudrashtakam (about 260k monthly searches combined, near-zero difficulty).
- Five scripture answers to film questions trending on Google: why Ravan kidnapped Sita, Ravan at the swayamvar, Surpanakha, Lakshman's avatar, Ravan's boon.
- Defects fixed: leaked writer's note on the film page, Sunderkand title typo, broken chaupai breadcrumb, misleading character portraits, stale news headline, app prompt on the app page.
- Daily monitor now reports rising Trends queries, new queries we appear for, and near misses.

## Next, ranked by expected impact

1. **Sunderkand page.** Google dropped /sunderkand from the index. It repeats the 60 doha pages word for word. Put the verses first with meanings on demand, add audio links, a Hindi-first FAQ (paath vidhi, benefits, rules), and keep linking every doha. About 170k monthly searches depend on it.
2. **Home page for "ramayana".** 628 impressions this week at position 12.6, 0 clicks. Add the story in brief by kand, a characters strip, versions and dating, and a film strip. Consider /ramayana-story.
3. **More stutis and paath pages.** Shri Ramchandra Kripalu (Vinay Patrika, needs a verified text), Ram ji ki aarti, Ramayan ji ki aarti, Hanuman ji ke 12 naam, Navdha bhakti, Ram Shalaka Prashnavali (interactive).
4. **Expand chaupai from 20 to 50+**, with themed lists (for courage, for exams, daily recitation) and a "Mangal Bhavan" song page that maps each sung line to its chaupai.
5. **Hindi versions of /tulsidas and /who-wrote-the-ramayana** ("tulsidas ka jivan parichay" alone is about 23k a month).
6. **App conversion on reading pages** (1 to 4% today). One consistent Play badge, no floating bar over verses, an iPhone fallback (39 iPhone visitors clicked an Android link).
7. **Design system pass.** One kand card style, kand index as a number grid, meanings under each verse on doha pages, consistent Hindi fonts, shorter footer, character and place templates with linked citations.
8. **Seasonal pages**, ready two weeks ahead: Vivah Panchami (December), Ram Navami, Hanuman Jayanti, Diwali 2027.

## Not worth chasing

Box office, cast gossip, film song lyrics (copyrighted), "watch online" for the TV serial.
