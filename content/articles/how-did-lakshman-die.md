# How did Lakshman die?

Lakshman was not killed by anyone. In Maharshi Valmiki's Uttara Kanda, Sarga 106, Shri Ram renounces him after he interrupts a private conversation to save others from Sage Durvasa's curse. Lakshman goes to the Saryu and restrains his senses and breath. Indra then takes him bodily to heaven, unseen by people.

## Shri Ram's promise to Kaal

Kaal, or Time, comes to Shri Ram as an ascetic. He asks to speak alone and sets a condition:

> चोदितो राजसिंहेन मुनिर्वाक्यमभाषत ।
> द्वन्द्वे होतत् प्रवक्तव्यं हितं वै यद्यवेक्षसे ॥ १२ ॥
> यः शृणोति निरीक्षेद् वा स वध्यो भविता तव ।
> भवेद् वै मुनिमुख्यस्य वचनं यद्यवेक्षसे ॥ १३ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 103, Shlokas 12 and 13

“Addressed by Shri Ram, the ascetic said: If you regard our welfare, we should speak where only we two are present. If you heed the chief ascetic's words, whoever hears us or sees us speaking must be put to death by you.”

Shri Ram agrees. He tells Lakshman to dismiss the door attendant and guard the entrance:

> स मे वध्यः खलु भवेद् वाचं द्वन्द्वसमीरितम् ।
> ऋषेर्मम च सौमित्रे पश्येद् वा शृणुयाच्च यः ॥ १५ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 103, Shloka 15

“O son of Sumitra, whoever sees or hears the conversation between the sage and Me shall indeed be killed by Me.”

## Durvasa at the door

While Lakshman stands guard, Sage Durvasa arrives. He demands to see Shri Ram immediately:

> अस्मिन् क्षणे मां सौमित्रे रामाय प्रतिवेदय ।
> अस्मिन् क्षणे मां सौमित्रे न निवेदयसे यदि ।
> विषयं त्वां पुरं चैव शपिष्ये राघवं तथा ॥ ६ ॥
> भरतं चैव सौमित्रे युष्माकं या च संततिः ।
> न हि शक्ष्याम्यहं भूयो मन्युं धारयितुं हृदि ॥ ७ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 105, Shlokas 6 and 7

“O son of Sumitra, announce me to Shri Ram this very moment. Otherwise, I shall curse the kingdom, the city, you, Shri Ram, Bharat and all your descendants. I can no longer contain my anger.”

Lakshman must choose between breaking Shri Ram's command and exposing everyone to the curse.

> एकस्य मरणं मेऽस्तु मा भूत् सर्वविनाशनम् ।
> इति बुद्ध्या विनिश्चित्य राघवाय न्यवेदयत् ॥ ९ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 105, Shloka 9

“Let me alone die. Let everyone else be spared destruction. Having firmly decided this, he announced Durvasa's arrival to Shri Ram.”

The word *एकस्य* means “of one alone”. Lakshman knowingly accepts the cost himself so that others may be spared. He enters and announces the sage.

## Lakshman asks Shri Ram to keep His word

Seeing Shri Ram bowed in sorrow, Lakshman speaks cheerfully and gently. He asks Him to honour His promise:

> न संतापं महाबाहो मदर्थं कर्तुमर्हसि ।
> पूर्वनिर्माणबद्धा हि कालस्य गतिरीदृशी ॥ २ ॥
> जहि मां सौम्य विस्रब्धं प्रतिज्ञां परिपालय ।
> हीनप्रतिज्ञाः काकुत्स्थ प्रयान्ति नरकं नराः ॥ ३ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 106, Shlokas 2 and 3

“O mighty-armed Lord, do not grieve for me. Such is Time's course, bound by deeds of past lives. Gracious Lord, put me to death and keep Your promise. Those who break their promises go to hell.”

He continues:

> यदि प्रीतिर्महाराज यद्यनुग्राह्यता मयि ।
> जहि मां निर्विशङ्कस्त्वं धर्मं वर्धय राघव ॥ ४ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 106, Shloka 4

“O King, if You love me and consider me worthy of Your grace, put me to death without hesitation and further dharma.”

Vasishtha advises Shri Ram to renounce Lakshman:

> त्यजैनं बलवान् कालो मा प्रतिज्ञां वृथा कृथाः ।
> प्रतिज्ञायां हि नष्टायां धर्मो हि विलयं व्रजेत् ॥ ९ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 106, Shloka 9

“Give him up. Time is powerful. Do not make Your promise false, for when the promise is broken, dharma will perish.”

Shri Ram follows this counsel:

> विसर्जये त्वां सौमित्रे मा भूद् धर्मविपर्ययः ।
> त्यागो वधो वा विहितः साधूनां ह्यभयं समम् ॥ १३ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 106, Shloka 13

“O son of Sumitra, I renounce you so that dharma may not suffer. For the good, being given up and being killed are considered the same.”

Shri Ram's words bring about their separation. He does not kill Lakshman.

## On the bank of the Saryu

With tears in his eyes, Lakshman leaves immediately. He goes straight to the Saryu without returning home.

> स गत्वा सरयूतीरमुपस्पृश्य कृताञ्जलिः ।
> निगृह्य सर्वस्रोतांसि निःश्वासं न मुमोच ह ॥ १५ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 106, Shloka 15

“Reaching the Saryu bank, he sipped water, folded his hands, restrained all his senses and held his breath.”

The gods, sages and apsaras shower flowers upon him. Indra then takes him to heaven:

> अदृश्यं सर्वमनुजैः सशरीरं महाबलम् ।
> प्रगृह्य लक्ष्मणं शक्रस्त्रिदिवं संविवेश ह ॥ १७ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 106, Shloka 17

“Taking the mighty Lakshman, still in his body and unseen by all people, Indra entered heaven.”

The word *सशरीरं* means “with his body”. Lakshman's departure is bodily, and the gods receive him with joy:

> ततो विष्णोश्चतुर्भागमागतं सुरसत्तमाः ।
> हृष्टाः प्रमुदिताः सर्वे पूजयन्ति स्म राघवम् ॥ १८ ॥
> Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda, Sarga 106, Shloka 18

“Then all the foremost gods, rejoicing at the arrival of Lakshman, the fourth part of Vishnu, honoured him.”

## What the Ramcharitmanas says

Goswami Tulsidas does not narrate Lakshman's departure in the Ramcharitmanas. His Uttar Kand ends with the joy of Shri Ram's reign and the glory of devotion.

## What Lakshman's choice teaches us

Lakshman places the safety of others before his own life. Even in the sorrow of separation, he asks Shri Ram to keep His word.

Our daily duties ask far less of us. If we promise to help a colleague finish a difficult task, keeping that promise may mean declining an enjoyable invitation. Someone has trusted our word and planned around it. Remembering that dependence can help us put our responsibility before our convenience.

Today, recall one promise someone is relying on. Take one concrete step to fulfil it.

## Questions people ask

### How did Lakshman die?

Lakshman leaves the world after Shri Ram renounces him. At the Saryu, he restrains his senses and breath, and Indra takes him bodily to heaven.

### Did Shri Ram kill Lakshman?

No. Shri Ram renounces Lakshman on Vasishtha's advice. Indra then takes Lakshman bodily to heaven.

### Where did Lakshman leave the world?

On the bank of the Saryu. He is taken in his body, so this account does not describe him leaving a body behind.

### Who came to meet Shri Ram when Lakshman stood guard?

Kaal comes as an ascetic for a private conversation. Sage Durvasa then arrives while Lakshman guards the door.
