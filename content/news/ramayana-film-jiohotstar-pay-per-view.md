JioHotstar and Prime Focus Studios have announced a partnership to bring [the Ramayana film](/ramayana-movie) to viewers in India as a pay-per-view title after its exclusive cinema window ends. PTI, The Hollywood Reporter India and Outlook reported the announcement on 21 and 22 October 2026.

Viewers will rent the film on demand. Outlook calls this a first for JioHotstar. The streaming date and the price have not been announced.

The film releases worldwide in cinemas on 5 November 2026 in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and English. Producer Namit Malhotra said it is best seen on the biggest screen, but cinemas cannot reach everyone.

## Receive the story with reverence

Bhagavan Shiv teaches Mata Parvati how to listen:

> रामकथा सुंदर कर तारी। संसय बिहग उड़ावनिहारी॥ रामकथा कलि बिटप कुठारी। सादर सुनु गिरिराजकुमारी॥
>
> Ramcharitmanas, Bal Kand, Chaupai 1.114.1

The story of Shri Ram drives away the birds of doubt like a lovely clap of the hands. It is an axe to the tree of Kali. The word *सादर* means “with reverence”. However we meet the story, in a cinema or at home, it asks to be received with respect.
