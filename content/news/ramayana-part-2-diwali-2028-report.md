Part 2 of “Ramayana: Rise of a Legend” will release at Diwali 2028 instead of Diwali 2027, producer Namit Malhotra has confirmed. NewsBytes reported on 4 November 2026 that Malhotra wants more time to complete the film and give it a grand finale.

The Hollywood Reporter India reports that the Diwali 2028 window appears in Part One's closing credits. No exact release date has been announced. Earlier reports from Bollywood Hungama in October 2026 had said the war portion needed more shooting time.

## Read the war in the scriptures

The war belongs to Lanka Kand in Goswami Tulsidas's [Ramcharitmanas](/ramcharitmanas) and Yuddha Kanda in Maharshi Valmiki's Ramayana. In Lanka Kand, the bridge across the sea leads to the war, Ravan's death and Shri Ram's return to Ayodhya. Reading these events in order gives each episode its place in the whole account.

Begin with [Lanka Kand](/ramcharitmanas/lanka-kand) or [Yuddha Kanda](/valmiki-ramayana/yuddha-kanda), and give a short passage your full attention each day. The free [Ramayan app](/app) offers a way to keep reading on the phone, with bookmarks to return to the passage where you stopped.
