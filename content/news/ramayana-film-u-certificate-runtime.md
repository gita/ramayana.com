**Ramayana film gets a U certificate, runtime about 3 hours 15 minutes**

Reports say the CBFC has granted the Ramayana film a U certificate, suitable for all ages. The same reports put its runtime at about 3 hours 15 minutes, or 195 minutes. These certification and runtime details remain unconfirmed.

One report also describes a shorter international English cut running about 2 hours 45 minutes, without song sequences. That version and its reported runtime remain unconfirmed.

The film opens on 5 November 2026. The reported runtimes refer to different cuts, so the shorter figure should not be read as a revision to the reported 195-minute version.

## Turn to the scripture

Shri Ram's life teaches us to uphold dharma when keeping our word demands sacrifice. In the [Ramcharitmanas](/ramcharitmanas), Goswami Tulsidas unfolds this teaching through the events of Ayodhya Kand. Shri Ram accepts fourteen years of exile, while Bharat refuses the throne. Each places duty above personal gain.
