Common questions

How do I change the language of the meaning? Open Settings and choose Meaning language.

How do I make the text larger? In the reader, tap the text size button, or open Settings and choose Text size.

How do I save a verse? While reading, tap the bookmark at the top. Saved verses are in Settings under Bookmarks.

Does the app need the internet? No. The full text is inside the app and works offline.

Where does the text come from? The Ramcharitmanas follows the Gita Press edition, and every source is credited in the app under Settings, About.
