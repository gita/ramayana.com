Privacy policy

Ramayan app (Ramcharitmanas and Valmiki Ramayana) and Ramayana.com. Last updated 22 October 2026.

The Ramayan app is made by Ved Vyas Foundation. It is free and has no ads. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What the app collects

Nothing that identifies you. The app does not ask for an account, does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location or photos, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, reading progress, bookmarks and streak are stored only on your device. They never leave it, and deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you share a verse, the app hands the text or image to your phone's own share sheet. We do not see what you share or who you share it with.

Ratings

If you choose to rate the app, the rating is handled by Google Play or the App Store under their privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds an optional account or anything else that handles personal data, we will update this page before that version is released and describe exactly what is collected and why.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@bhagavadgita.io.
